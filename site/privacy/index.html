Notabo privacy

How the device-hosted planner uses Google Calendar.

This policy covers the Google Calendar connection in the device-hosted version of Notabo.

With your permission, Notabo reads calendar names so you can choose which calendars to use. It can read, create, change and delete events on calendars you own. Your selections in Notabo limit which calendars it uses, although Google's permission covers all calendars you own.

Notabo uses this data to show your events and sync your plans with Google Calendar when you choose to sync. The calendar data it keeps is stored on your own devices. Sync sends your changes to Google Calendar, where Google also stores your events. We never sell this data or share it with anyone else.

Your connection credentials are kept in your own 1Password account.

You can revoke Notabo's access at Google Account permissions. Revoking access stops future access to Google Calendar; it does not erase copies already stored on your devices.

For privacy questions, write to ciao@abcastor.com.
